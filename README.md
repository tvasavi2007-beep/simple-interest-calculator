# Simple Interest Calculator

## Description
A simple project to calculate interest based on the principal amount, rate of interest, and time period.

## Formula
Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

## Inputs
- Principal amount
- Rate of interest
- Time period in years

## Example
Principal = 1000
Rate = 5%
Time = 2 years

Simple Interest = (1000 × 5 × 2) / 100 = 100
